Juliette Nichols

Image Source: https://silo.fandom.com/wiki/Juliette_Nichols

From the book and TV series "SILO", Juliette Nichols is a character known for her intelligence and resourcefulness. She is a determined admirable young woman fighting for the good of the people living within the silo. Although she faces many challenges, she always rises above them with courage and humility

  • Juliette Nichols shows bravery despite difficult situations.
  • Juliette Nichols always questions what is presented to here in pursuit of the truth.
  • Juliette Nichols shows kindness and compassion to those in need.
