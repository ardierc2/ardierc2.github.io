Nocturnal animals have evolved for true darkness, Artificial light at Night disrupts this evolution. From how they mate, to how they hunt and navigate. Many can't adapt fast enough.

  • Sea turtle hatchlings crawl toward city lights instead of the ocean.
  • Fireflies can't read each other's flash signals.
  • Bats, owls, and moths lose the dark they depend on to hunt and find shelter.

From the coast to the desert, no habitat has gone untouched.

Why Lights Out?

Most resources about Artificial Light at Night (ALAN) and light pollution focus on how they affect astronomy. Lights Out shows who they hurt. Explore animals by habitat to see how ALAN affects each one and learn how you can help.
